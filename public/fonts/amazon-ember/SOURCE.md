# Amazon Ember font provenance

Downloaded 9 November 2026 from the official Amazon typography page:
https://www.developer.amazon.com/en-US/alexa/branding/echo-guidelines/identity-guidelines/typography

Official archive:
https://m.media-amazon.com/images/G/01/mobile-apps/dex/alexa/branding/Amazon_Typefaces_Complete_Font_Set_Mar2020.zip

These are unmodified AmazonEmber_Rg.ttf and AmazonEmber_Bd.ttf, renamed for
readability. The archive's Amazon Ember Licensing Guidelines.pdf is preserved
here as Usage-Guidelines.pdf. Its overview states that including these designs
requires no licence payments, royalties or additional attribution.
The proper family name remains Amazon Ember; embedded copyright data is intact.
Use does not imply endorsement or partnership with Amazon.
