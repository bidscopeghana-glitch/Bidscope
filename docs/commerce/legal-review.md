# Editable customer policy drafts — NOT APPROVED

## Terms
Contracting seller: [legal entity, company number, postal address, support contact].
BidScope role: [reseller / agent / marketplace, as established by signed agreements].
The seller remains responsible for its customer-facing obligations. Supplier
fulfilment does not automatically transfer those obligations to CJ or Alibaba.
Complete pricing, permitted payment methods, acceptance/cancellation rules,
applicable law and dispute handling before publication.

## Privacy
Identify the controller and contact. Describe account, payment-reference, delivery,
tracking and support data; purposes and lawful basis; actual payment and fulfilment
recipients; international transfers; retention; access/deletion/complaint procedures.
Do not collect card numbers. Complete processor contracts and Ghana privacy review.

## Shipping
Quote origin, supported destination and service, estimated processing and transit
separately. Confirm customs documentation, importer of record, duty/tax payer,
delivery failure handling and split shipment charges. Estimates are not guarantees.

## Returns and refunds
Agree item eligibility, exclusions, request deadline, return address, responsibility
for return shipping and defective/lost goods, approval process and refund method.
Keep requested, approved, submitted and provider-confirmed refunds distinct.
Do not promise supplier buyer protection without verified agreement coverage.
