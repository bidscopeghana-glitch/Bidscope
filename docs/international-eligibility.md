# International eligibility policy

BidScope does not equate a public notice with supplier eligibility. International records default to `UNCLEAR`. They become `INTERNATIONAL_ELIGIBLE` only when official text explicitly signals international competition, worldwide participation, or all eligible countries. Invitation-only, shortlisted and prequalified procedures are `RESTRICTED`.

Cards and detail pages must show the stored `eligibility_summary` and link to the official notice. Users must verify nationality, establishment, sanctions, qualification, local-registration, tax, bid-security and place-of-performance rules before investing in a bid. Automated match scores are discovery aids, not legal or procurement advice.
