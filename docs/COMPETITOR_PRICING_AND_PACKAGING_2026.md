# BidScope competitor pricing and package strategy

Research date: 15 September 2026

## Executive recommendation

BidScope should not compete as a cheaper copy of a tender-listing website. Its strongest defensible position is Ghana-first procurement intelligence: discover the opportunity, monitor it, understand the buyer and prior awards, decide whether to bid, and organise the work.

The recommended catalogue is Free plus three paid packages:

| Package | Monthly | Annual | Primary job |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Free | GHS 0 | GHS 0 | Inspect live opportunities and verify official sources |
| Professional | GHS 295 | GHS 2,950 | Monitor matches, alerts, deadlines, buyers and changes |
| Intelligence | GHS 450 | GHS 4,500 | Qualify bids using awards, buyer intelligence, readiness and AI |
| Business | GHS 600 | GHS 6,000 | Run a repeatable bid pipeline with the full workspace and higher limits |

Annual pricing includes roughly two months without charge. At the Bank of Ghana 14 September 2026 USD/GHS mid-rate of 11.4630, the annual paid prices are approximately USD 257, USD 393 and USD 523. This puts BidScope close to GhanaTenders' current annual price anchors while preserving a lower-risk monthly entry route.

## Why this structure is stronger

1. **It prices outcomes, not public information.** Free users can verify notices. Paid users buy monitoring, prioritisation, analysis and workflow.
2. **It creates a natural upgrade path.** Professional solves "do not miss it"; Intelligence solves "is it worth bidding"; Business solves "how do we deliver the bid".
3. **It is locally payable.** Prices remain in Ghana cedis and can use Paystack rather than forcing a USD purchase.
4. **It avoids undeliverable promises.** A solo operator should not sell 24/7 support or dedicated account management until staffing and service levels exist.
5. **It preserves room for a future Team package.** Seat invites, roles, activity history and team-level alerts should be completed before a five-seat offer is launched.

## Features worth adapting next

### Highest priority

- Hosted document library with reliable source provenance, virus scanning and download history.
- Team invitations, seat limits, member roles and removal controls.
- Alert delivery to multiple business email addresses.
- CSV/Excel exports for saved opportunities, awards and pipeline data.
- Briefing/site-visit reminders alongside closing deadlines.
- Competitor award profiles: wins, buyers, sectors, award values and trends.

### Valuable differentiation

- Downloadable AI submission checklist tied to the official bid documents.
- Amendment comparison showing what changed and what action is required.
- Procurement-plan radar that connects planned procurement to later tender notices.
- A referral programme that awards additional subscription time after a successful paid referral.
- Optional analyst/onboarding service sold separately, rather than pretending it is included for every subscriber.

### Defer

- WhatsApp alerts until a provider, consent flow, opt-out and delivery monitoring are operational.
- API access until rate limits, keys, audit logging and a commercial support policy exist.
- "Unlimited downloads" until BidScope actually hosts and controls a safe document-delivery pipeline.

## Local implementation prepared

- Rebuilt the public plans interface around Free, Professional, Intelligence and Business.
- Added monthly/annual switching and a full comparison table.
- Added backend plan definitions, feature flags and usage limits for six paid billing records.
- Restricted checkout validation to the new plan codes.
- Kept all new paid checkouts disabled until the proposed catalogue is approved and matching Paystack plans are intentionally created.
- Preserved legacy plans and existing transactions rather than deleting financial history.

The proposed catalogue is deliberately local-only at this stage. It should be published only after the owner approves the package names, prices and feature allocation.
